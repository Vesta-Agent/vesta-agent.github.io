Release notes

iPhone

Use Vesta on your iPhone for free.

There is no App Store app. Install Vesta as a web app from Safari and connect it to Vesta Agent running on your Windows PC (on the same Wi-Fi or through Tailscale). You get chat, voice and notifications. Phone control is not available on iPhone.

1. On your PC, open Vesta Agent > Settings > Phone connection and note the address (or scan the QR code).

2. On the iPhone, open Safari and go to that address (it must use https when you are away from home, for example through Tailscale).

3. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

4. Open the new icon and finish setup. Chat, voice and notifications work; the agent still uses tools on your PC.

Tip: the same address also works as a bookmark in any browser.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I get help or report a bug?

Message our support bot on Telegram, @VestaAgentSupportBot. Send a description, screenshots or a voice message and you get a ticket number. Replies arrive in the same chat.

Is Vesta Agent free?

Yes. The Windows and Android apps are free, and there is a free iPhone web app that connects to Vesta on your PC. You use your own AI key, for example a free Gemini or OpenRouter key, or run models offline with Ollama.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your own device: chats, memory, agents, settings and encrypted keys. There is no Vesta chat server. Optional anonymous usage stats (a random install id, version, OS, language, country, device type and time in the app) can be turned off in Settings.

Does it work in Iran?

Yes. Add a proxy in Settings, for example the local port from v2rayN or NekoBox, and choose which services use it. A built-in guide shows each step.

Which AI models does it support?

Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI (Grok), OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Groq, Mistral, Together, NVIDIA NIM, any OpenAI-compatible provider, and local models through Ollama or LM Studio.

Will it update automatically?

Yes. It checks quietly at launch and only shows a window when a new version is available, then downloads, verifies and installs it for you.

Can I keep chatting while the agent works?

Yes. Since 1.7 long tasks run in the background of their chat. Ask how it is going, add or change instructions, or cancel it, without stopping the work.

Who makes Vesta Agent?

Persian Studio, founded by SOBi and Mahan.

Why does Windows show a blue warning?

The installer is not yet signed with a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Privacy

Your data never leaves home.

  • No Vesta chat servers and no online database of your conversations. Chats, memory and settings live on your device. Optional anonymous usage stats (version, OS, language, country, device type and time in the app) can be turned off, and then nothing is sent.
  • Occasional sponsored cards come from a signed feed and never see your chats. Hide any card for 7 days with one tap.
  • API keys are encrypted locally. Passwords, keys and card details are never saved to memory.
  • Only the message you send goes to the AI provider whose key you added. With Ollama, nothing leaves at all.
  • A visible indicator is always on while the microphone or screen sharing is active.
